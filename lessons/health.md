# Health and Illness: Simple English Vocabulary

## Feelings (general state)

* **I feel fine / great.** — My body and mood are good. I am okay.
* **I feel sick / ill.** — I am not well. My body feels bad.
* **I feel terrible.** — I feel very, very bad.
* **I feel weak / tired / exhausted.** — I have no energy. I need rest. "Exhausted" means very, very tired.
* **I feel dizzy.** — My head feels like it is spinning. I may fall.
* **I feel cold.** — My body feels cold. I may be shaking.
* **I feel hot.** — My body feels very warm. Maybe I have a fever.
* **I'm better / worse today.** — Today my health is good / bad compared to before.
* **I'm recovering.** — I am getting better. My health is coming back.

## Pain (where it hurts)

* **I have a headache / toothache / backache / stomach ache.** — I feel pain in my head / tooth / back / stomach.
* **My throat is sore.** — My throat hurts. It feels painful.
* **My nose is stuffy / runny.** — "Stuffy" means I cannot breathe through my nose. "Runny" means liquid comes out of my nose.
* **It hurts when I swallow / breathe / move.** — I feel pain when I do this action.

## Symptoms

* **a cough (dry / wet)** — A cough is when air comes out of your mouth fast and loud. "Dry" means no mucus. "Wet" means with mucus.
* **a high temperature / a fever** — Your body is hotter than normal.
* **a broken arm / a bruise / a cut** — "Broken" means the bone is damaged. "Bruise" means the skin is dark and hurts after a hit. "Cut" means the skin is open.
* **itch** (verb) / **itchy** (adjective) — "Itch" is the feeling. "Itchy" describes something that makes you want to scratch.
* **My arm itches.** — My arm feels like I need to scratch it.
* **My skin is itchy.** — My skin feels like I need to scratch it.

### 4. Correct the mistake — answers

1. He has a **wet** cough with a lot of mucus. — *"Dry" doesn't go with mucus.*
2. She was **bitten** by a mosquito.
3. My nose is **stuffy**. I can't breathe through it. — *"Runny" means liquid comes out, not blocked.*
4. I need to **take** a painkiller. *Or:* I need to put **cream** on my skin. — *You swallow a painkiller.*
5. I feel better today. Yesterday I was **worse**. — *You can't say "more worse".*
6. I have a **stomach ache** (or: I have a headache **and** a stomach ache). — *You can't say "headache in my stomach".*
7. The bee **sting** is really itchy. I can't stop scratching. — *A bee stings, it doesn't bite.*
8. I feel **hot** (or: I have a fever). My body is very hot. — *"Dizzy" is a spinning head, not heat.*
9. I have a pimple on my forehead. **I feel exhausted** (or: It's very **itchy**). — *A pimple can't be "exhausted".*
10. My throat is sore. It hurts when I **swallow** (or: **drink tea**). — *You can't "breathe tea".*

1. **Things that hurt:** headache, toothache, backache, stomach ache, sore throat, bee sting, cut, bruise.
2. **Things on the skin:** pimple, spot, bruise, cut, rash, mosquito bite, bee sting.
3. **Things you do when you are sick:** see a doctor, take medicine, stay in bed, rest, drink warm tea, take a day off.
4. **Things a doctor gives you:** medicine, pills, a painkiller.
5. **Words to describe how you feel:** fine, great, sick, ill, terrible, weak, tired, exhausted, dizzy, cold, hot, better, worse.

### 18. Mini-story — sample

Yesterday I felt terrible. I had a headache and a high temperature. My nose was stuffy and I had a sore throat. I felt weak and exhausted. I stayed in bed and drank plenty of water. I took a painkiller and took a day off. Today I feel better. I'm recovering. I hope I get well soon.





### 19. Discussion questions — samples

1. When I have a cold, I stay in bed, drink warm tea, and take medicine.
2. Yes, I had food poisoning once. My stomach hurt and I felt sick. I saw a doctor.
3. A sore throat is the worst for me because it hurts when I swallow.
4. Yes, I take a day off because I need rest and I don't want other people to get sick.
5. I say: "Get well soon! Take care!"
6. Yes, a bee stung me. It was painful. The spot was itchy.
7. When my skin is itchy, I scratch it. But I try to stop.
8. I know I'm recovering when I feel better and I have more energy.

### 20. Put the dialogue in order — answers

**Correct order:**

C → E → B → F → G → D → A

That is:

**C:** Hi! How do you feel?
**E:** I feel terrible. I have a high temperature and a sore throat.
**B:** Do you have a cough?
**F:** Yes, a dry cough. And my nose is stuffy.
**G:** You should see a doctor and stay in bed.
**D:** Thank you. I will take a day off today.
**A:** Get well soon!
