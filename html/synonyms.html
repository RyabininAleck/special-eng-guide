ENGLISH VOCABULARY · SYNONYMS

20 synonym families (B2) + Present Perfect in review

Synonyms:
same idea, different weight

In English, important is only the middle of the scale. Crucial means “if this fails, everything fails.” Significant means “big enough to show in numbers.” Essential means “you cannot do without it.” You learn which word fits which situation, not a flat list.

For teachers

20 groups by base word: cards → Gap → Match. Typing only. Present Perfect block uses have/has + V3 with synonym verbs. Progress key: synonyms-v1 in localStorage.

PP + synonyms: We have reduced costs (result relevant now) vs We reduced costs last year (closed time).

I. Where English gets so many near-synonyms

English is layered: a Germanic core (ask), Latin borrowings (require), French polish (demand). “Synonyms” are often not equal. They differ in strength (big → enormous), register (chat vs report), and collocation (make a decision, not do a decision).

Start with the plain base word (important, big). Then upgrade for the situation — email, PR, code review. The goal is use, not memorizing a list for a test.

II. Three levers — strength, register, collocation

LeverQuestionExample
StrengthHow strong is the claim?important → crucial
RegisterSpeech, email, or legal text?stop → cease / discontinue
CollocationWhat word usually goes next?significant increase · adverse effect

One idea in your head may need different English upgrades in different contexts.

Grammar bridge

Present Perfect + synonym verbs

Present Perfect links change to now: We have increased sales — numbers are still high; They have ceased production — the plant is still shut. A closed time → Past Simple: Sales surged last year.

We have + expanded / reduced / modified / disclosed …
Has the team + adopted / terminated … yet?
I have never + facilitated / attended … (experience)
