# Interesting Words

---

### Until: T1 = T2 (thermal equilibrium)

**Meaning:** Until the temperatures of two systems become equal — the state when heat stops flowing between them.

**Examples:**
- The heat transfer continues **until thermal equilibrium is reached**.
- Leave the hot water and cold water in contact **until T1 = T2**.
- Two bodies in thermal contact exchange energy **until thermal equilibrium**.

---

### convection currents

**Meaning:** The flow of liquid or gas caused by the difference in temperature — warmer fluid rises, cooler fluid sinks, creating a circular movement.

**Examples:**
- Heating water from below **creates convection currents** that distribute heat.
- **Convection currents** in the Earth's mantle cause tectonic plates to move.
- The fan was unnecessary because **convection currents** already circulated the warm air.

---

### concentrated on a very small edge

**Meaning:** Force, pressure, or focus applied to a narrow, limited area — a small point or line of contact.

**Examples:**
- The entire weight of the building is **concentrated on a very small edge** of the foundation.
- She **concentrated** her efforts **on a very small edge** of the problem.
- The pressure is too high because it is **concentrated on a very small edge**.

---

### particles are far apart and move freely

**Meaning:** A description of gas — one of the states of matter. Gas particles have weak intermolecular bonds, so they spread out and travel in random directions.

**Examples:**
- In a **gas, particles are far apart and move freely**, which is why gases fill any container.
- Unlike solids, **gas particles are far apart and move freely**, making gases easily compressible.
- At high temperatures, molecules behave like a **gas — particles are far apart and move freely**.

---

### surface

**Meaning:** The outer layer or top part of something; the boundary between a solid and a fluid (liquid or gas).

**Examples:**
- The water **surface** was perfectly calm before the storm.
- Heat is transferred through the **surface** of the metal by conduction.
- The **surface** area of the object affects how quickly it cools down.
- Ice forms on the **surface** of a lake when the temperature drops.
