# Physics Vocabulary Learning Plan

## Goal

Build physics vocabulary in English step by step from zero, with a strong focus on understanding concepts, using words in context, and speaking clearly about physics.

## Main Principle

Do not memorize isolated words. Learn vocabulary together with simple physical ideas, examples, and active practice. This will help you remember the words and use them naturally.

## Visual Learning

Use graphics, diagrams, schemes, and charts to explain physics concepts. Visual aids help understanding and memory. Each lesson should include relevant diagrams where possible.

## Weekly Structure

Use this schedule:

1. Day 1: learn 10-15 new words
2. Day 2: read examples and short explanations
3. Day 3: write your own sentences
4. Day 4: do mini speaking practice
5. Day 5: read a short physics text
6. Day 6: review old vocabulary
7. Day 7: test yourself

## How to Study Each Word

For every new word, learn these six things:

1. definition
2. pronunciation
3. example sentence
4. related words
5. one sentence of your own
6. a simple diagram or sketch (if possible)

Example:

- `force`
- Definition: a push or pull that can change motion
- Example: "A greater force causes greater acceleration."
- Related words: `mass`, `motion`, `acceleration`
- Your sentence: "Force changes the speed of an object."

## Best Starting Point

If you are starting from zero, begin with this order:

1. measurement
2. matter
3. mass
4. volume
5. density
6. motion
7. speed
8. velocity
9. acceleration
10. force
11. energy
12. temperature

This first block is important because these words appear in almost every part of physics.

## First Two-Week Goal

For the first two weeks, focus only on:
- basic quantities
- motion
- force
- energy
- temperature

This will give you a strong foundation before moving to more specialized topics.
