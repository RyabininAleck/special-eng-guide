## Task 1 — Fill in `to` where needed (or write *nothing* if not needed)

a) I moved _____ Hangzhou last year.  
b) She sent the motivation letter _____ the admissions office.  
c) Can you explain this algorithm _____ me?  
d) I look forward _____ (hear) from the university.  
e) We must submit the form today.

---

## Task 2 — Choose the correct sentence

a) I prefer working in startups to working in banks.  
b) I prefer working in startups than working in banks.

---

## Task 3 — Correct the errors

a) > Please listen me carefully.

b) > I am similar with your approach.

c) > He is glad meeting you.

## Task 4 — Label each `to` as **infinitive**, **preposition**, or **purpose**

1. I **want to** apply **to** ZJU.
2. She flew **to** Beijing **to** attend a conference.
3. I look forward **to starting** my degree.

*(Write: sentence 1 — …, sentence 2 — …, etc.)*

---

## Task 5 — Write two sentences:

1. One with `to` as a **preposition** before a person (`to me / to the professor`).
2. One with `to` as a **preposition** before `-ing` (`look forward to` or `be used to`).
