# Lesson: Natural Non-Question Invitations

If you speak to a beautiful girl, the safest natural non-question invitations are:

- Come over sometime.
- You should come over sometime.
- I'd love to have you over for coffee sometime.
- Join me for coffee tomorrow.


Use this pattern: join me for + activity
Examples:
• Join me for coffee.                                                                                                                                     
• Join me for dinner.                                                                                                                                     
• Join me for a walk.
