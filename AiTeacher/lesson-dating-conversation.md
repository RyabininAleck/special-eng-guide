# Lesson: Dating Conversation

## Topic

How to start, continue, and gently develop a dating conversation in natural English.

## Short Conversation

Man: Hi, I really enjoy talking with you. You have a very calm energy.

Woman: Thank you. That's nice to hear.

Man: I'd love to get to know you better. Join me for coffee sometime.

Woman: That sounds nice.

Man: Great. We could meet tomorrow evening if you're free.

Woman: Yes, tomorrow works for me.
