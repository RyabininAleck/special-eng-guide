# Exercises: "To" Between Verbs and After Verbs

**Lesson:** [lesson-verb-to-patterns.md](./lesson-verb-to-patterns.md)  
**Answers:** [answers-verb-to-patterns.md](./answers-verb-to-patterns.md)

Fill in the blank. Use `to`, a bare verb, or the correct `-ing` / `to + verb` form from the brackets.

---

## §1 — To-Infinitive: `verb (+ object) + to + verb`

1. She decided _____ apply for a CS program at ZJU.
2. I want _____ specialize in artificial intelligence.
3. We plan _____ move to Hangzhou next year.
4. He hopes _____ pass the language exam.
5. They agreed _____ collaborate on the robotics project.
6. The professor asked me _____ revise the introduction.
7. The program requires students _____ maintain a high GPA.
8. She encouraged us _____ participate in the hackathon.
9. I need _____ improve my academic writing.
10. He managed _____ deploy the update before the deadline.

---

## §2 — Bare Infinitive: no `to`

1. You should _____ (practice) speaking every day.
2. We must _____ (submit) the application before Friday.
3. I can _____ (explain) the system architecture clearly.
4. They will _____ (release) the firmware update tomorrow.
5. The supervisor let me _____ (present) my research proposal.
6. The misconfiguration made the service _____ (crash).
7. I saw the race car _____ (stop) at the pit lane.
8. We heard the engine _____ (fail) during the stress test.
9. You might _____ (need) more data for this model.
10. She could _____ (handle) the telemetry integration herself.

---

## §3 — `verb + -ing` Instead of `to`

1. I enjoy _____ (solve) complex algorithmic problems.
2. He avoided _____ (mention) his previous employer.
3. We finished _____ (implement) the REST API.
4. She suggested _____ (apply) for a research internship.
5. I practice _____ (speak) English every morning.
6. They recommended _____ (use) a more secure protocol.
7. He keeps _____ (check) the logs even when nothing is wrong.
8. Would you mind _____ (wait) outside for a moment?
9. She denied _____ (delete) the production database.
10. I miss _____ (work) on engineering projects with that team.

---

## §4 — `-ing` vs `to`: Different Meanings

1. I stopped _____ (work) at the bank in 2023.
2. We stopped _____ (check) the error logs on the server.
3. I remember _____ (meet) the admissions officer in Shanghai.
4. Remember _____ (send) your motivation letter today.
5. I'll never forget _____ (present) at my first tech conference.
6. Don't forget _____ (back up) your code before merging.
7. Try _____ (restart) the service — that often fixes it.
8. I tried _____ (fix) the memory leak all night.
9. She went on _____ (talk) about distributed systems for an hour.
10. After the overview, he went on _____ (explain) the core algorithm.

---

## §5 — `to` for Purpose (in order to)

1. I moved to China _____ (study) computer science.
2. We refactored the module _____ (improve) code readability.
3. He attended the lecture _____ (learn) about forecasting models.
4. She woke up early _____ (prepare) for the admissions interview.
5. They added monitoring _____ (detect) failures faster.
6. I took detailed notes _____ (remember) the key formulas.
7. We joined the motorsport team _____ (gain) hands-on engineering experience.
8. He simplified the diagram _____ (make) it easier to understand.
9. I practice speaking daily _____ (build) confidence for university.
10. She saved money _____ (afford) the language year program.

## Task 2 — Mixed Review (all rules)

Fill in the blank. Rules from §1–§6 are mixed.

1. They refused _____ accept the offer without a written contract.
2. I look forward to _____ (start) my CS program in September.
3. You must _____ (complete) all prerequisite courses first.
4. He considered _____ (transfer) to a research-focused track.
5. I stopped _____ (work) on freelance projects after I joined the team.
6. I read the paper twice _____ (understand) the proof.
7. The advisor told him _____ rewrite the methodology section.
8. The memory leak made the application _____ (crash) during peak load.
9. She is used to _____ (work) under tight release deadlines.
10. I risked _____ (deploy) on Friday without full regression testing.
11. Try _____ (use) a binary search instead of a linear scan.
12. We appear _____ have underestimated the integration effort.
13. I watched the engineer _____ (install) the new telemetry module.
14. He flew to Hangzhou _____ (visit) the campus before applying.
15. I regret _____ (ignore) that security warning last month.
16. Would you like me _____ help you prepare for the interview?
17. He objected to _____ (change) the API contract midway.
18. They finished _____ (calibrate) the sensors on the race car.
19. Could you let us _____ (access) the server room after hours?
20. Don't forget _____ (attach) your transcript to the application.
